Complete Market Taxonomy

All Items Divided into 5 Market Categories

Every product in your home belongs to one of these 5 categories, each with distinct repair potential, resale demand, and eco-recycling rules.

📱

1. Electronics

Phones, Laptops, Mixers, TVs, Chargers

High technology depreciation, but immense repair value for cracked screens and dead batteries. If dead, safe e-waste recycling is legally mandatory to recover precious metals.

Optimal Route: Repair screen/battery → Resell if <3 yrs old → Certified E-Waste Recycle if dead.
🛋️

2. Furniture & Home (Wooden Items)

Chairs, Tables, Beds, Almirahs, Desks

Solid wood items retain high structural integrity. Polishing, re-upholstery, or joint-tightening costs under ₹500–₹1,200 and lasts a decade. Great demand for donation to schools and shelters.

Optimal Route: Local carpenter polish → Resell solid wood → Donate to rural education centers.
📚

3. Books & Educations

Textbooks, Reference Guides, Novels, Stationery

Knowledge never depreciates. School and college textbooks have the highest social impact when donated to needy students. Competitive exam books carry 50% resale value.

Optimal Route: Donate to Student Library Hubs → Sell exam guides → Pulp recycle only if torn.
🧸

4. Toys & Sports Items

Cycles, Cricket Kits, Board Games, Action Toys

Kids outgrow toys and sports gear rapidly. Bicycles and sports equipment are easily serviced (new brake pads/chains) and bring immediate joy to orphanages or sports academies.

Optimal Route: Free NGO child donation → Service cycle brakes/gears → Resell sports gear.
👕

5. Clothing & Daily Goods

Wearables, Bed Linen, Curtains, Household Utilities

Textile waste is a leading landfill pollutant. Clean, wearable clothes provide immediate winter relief. Damaged textiles should be routed to industrial fiber recycling.

Optimal Route: Doorstep NGO clothes drive → Upcycle into tote bags → Fiber recycling.

Common Questions

What if my phone screen is broken but the phone still turns on?

This is the prime candidate for the Repair option. A screen replacement usually costs ₹900–₹1,400 and restores full function for another 2+ years, saving you from spending ₹15,000+ on a new device.

How can I donate wooden furniture or old textbooks in Wankaner?

Select the Donate option to view registered NGO collection points like Yuva Vikas Seva Trust. For large wooden furniture and bulk textbook bundles, volunteer vans schedule a free doorstep pickup.

Is my personal data safe if I sell or recycle electronics?

Always perform a full factory reset before handing over any device. For recycling, our verified CPCB recyclers run physical shredding on storage chips to prevent any chance of recovery.
